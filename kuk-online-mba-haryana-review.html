Kurukshetra University (KUK) Online MBA: Detailed Review, Fees, & NAAC A+ Status

Direct Summary: Detailed analysis of Kurukshetra University's Online MBA program in Haryana. Explore total tuition fees under ₹1 Lakh, NAAC A+ grade, UGC-DEB accreditation, semester syllabus, and corporate job validity.

Established in 1956 by the President of India, Kurukshetra University (KUK) stands as Haryana's premier state government institution. Spanning over seven decades of academic heritage, Kurukshetra University launched its UGC-DEB approved online learning programs to make world-class management education accessible to students and working professionals across India.

In this detailed review, we evaluate the Kurukshetra University Online MBA across key parameters including accreditation, fee structure, syllabus flexibility, LMS platform, and placement assistance.

Prestige & Accreditation Check

Kurukshetra University Online is backed by prestigious statutory approvals:

  • NAAC A+ Accreditation: Assessed and accredited with an 'A+' grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council.
  • UGC-DEB Approved: Fully authorized by the Distance Education Bureau of the University Grants Commission to deliver online postgraduate degrees.
  • 70+ Years Legacy: State government university charter ensuring complete legal equivalence with full-time physical degrees.
  • Valid for Govt Jobs: 100% valid for UPSC, Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC), SSC, Bank PO, and NET exams.

Fee Structure & EMI Options

One of KUK Online MBA's biggest strengths is its budget affordability. The total program cost is kept strictly under ₹1 Lakh, making it an exceptional ROI choice for learners.

  • Total Tuition Fee: Approx ₹74,000 - ₹90,000 (payable semester-wise).
  • Monthly EMI Starts At: Approx ₹1,540/month via banking partners.
  • Examination Fees: Included / minimal nominal charges per semester.

Course Structure & Specializations

The Online MBA program spans 2 years (4 semesters) with a credit-based framework. Students can choose from key industry-focused specializations:

  • Financial Management: Corporate finance, investment analysis, banking systems, and portfolio management.
  • Marketing Management: Consumer behavior, digital marketing strategy, brand architecture, and market research.
  • Human Resource Management: Talent acquisition, organizational behavior, labor laws, and strategic HR leadership.
  • International Business: Global trade compliance, cross-cultural negotiation, and international supply chain management.

Learning Management System (LMS) & Examinations

KUK Online provides a user-friendly Learning Management System rated 4.4/5 by enrolled learners. Features include:

  • Self-Paced Recorded Video Modules by experienced university professors.
  • Interactive Live Weekend Sessions for doubt clearance and case study discussions.
  • Digital E-Library Access containing downloadable e-books, research papers, and lecture notes.
  • Online Proctored Exams allowing candidates to attempt end-term assessments safely from their homes.

With an average placement assistance rate of 84%, KUK Online supports graduates through resume writing workshops, mock interview modules, and career portal updates. Top hiring partners include:

  • KPMG
  • Cognizant
  • Amazon
  • Paytm
  • Infosys
  • ICICI Bank

Final Verdict
