Do Top Tech Firms Hire Grads with Online BCA or MCA Degrees? 2026 Industry Report

Direct Summary: Comprehensive 2026 tech recruitment report: How Amazon, Infosys, TCS, Capgemini, and startups evaluate candidates holding UGC-approved Online BCA and Online MCA degrees.

Quick Answer for IT Aspirants:

Yes, premier technology companies (including Amazon, Microsoft, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, and Capgemini) actively hire graduates with online BCA and MCA degrees from UGC-DEB and AICTE approved universities. Corporate tech screening relies primarily on coding assessments (HackerRank, LeetCode), technical system design interviews, and GitHub portfolio verification; statutory online degrees satisfy all formal HR qualification criteria without salary penalties.

How the Tech Industry Evaluates Online Computer Science Degrees

The tech sector has undergone a massive transformation. With the rise of skills-based hiring, automated applicant tracking systems (ATS), and remote work culture, recruiters care far more about a candidate's practical programming competence than whether they sat in a physical classroom.

However, misconceptions persist regarding whether an Online BCA or Online MCA holds equal ground against conventional on-campus degrees during corporate hiring. Here is what engineering hiring managers and HR directors report in 2026.

The 4-Stage IT Candidate Screening Process

  1. Stage 1: ATS Resume Screening & Statutory Check: Recruiter ATS systems verify that the candidate possesses a recognized Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Applications. Degrees from UGC-DEB recognized universities pass compliance checks automatically.
  2. Stage 2: Standardized Coding Assessment: Candidates complete algorithmic and data structures challenges on platforms like HackerRank, Codility, or LeetCode. Evaluation is 100% objective and performance-driven.
  3. Stage 3: Technical System Design & Live Interview: Engineers evaluate the candidate's understanding of databases (SQL/NoSQL), web architectures, cloud platforms (AWS/Azure), and clean code principles.
  4. Stage 4: Background Verification (BGV): Corporate background screening agencies (First Advantage, HireRight) verify UGC accreditation directly from university student databases.
