Frequently Asked Questions: Online Online MCA

Clear answers on approvals, eligibility, fees, exams, and career validity.

Can non-maths or commerce/arts graduates pursue an Online MCA?
Yes! Top online universities offer foundational bridge courses in mathematics and computer fundamentals during the first semester, allowing graduates from B.Com, BA, and BBA streams to enroll and switch into software engineering.
What are the most popular specializations in Online MCA?
Leading specializations include Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Data Science, and Full Stack Web Development.
What is the average fee for an Online MCA program in India?
The total fee for an Online MCA ranges between ₹1,10,000 and ₹1,80,000 across 2 years, with monthly EMI options starting at ₹3,000 to ₹4,500/month.
Is Online MCA recognized by AICTE and UGC-DEB?
Yes, technical master's degrees like Online MCA require joint approval from AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) and UGC-DEB for statutory validity in India.
What are the placement opportunities after an Online MCA?
Graduates are hired for roles including Full-Stack Developer, Cloud Architect, Data Analyst, and DevOps Engineer, with hiring partners like Infosys, TCS, Capgemini, Amazon, and Cognizant.
