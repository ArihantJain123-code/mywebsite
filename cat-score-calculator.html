CAT 2026 Answer Key & Objection Challenge Process

The official CAT provisional answer key is released alongside the candidate response sheet at iimcat.ac.in within 3 to 4 days of the exam. Candidates can raise objections during the 3-day challenge window by paying INR 1,200 per question, which is 100% refunded if the objection is validated.

If you discover discrepancies or ambiguities in the official provisional answer key for Slot 1, Slot 2, or Slot 3, you can file an objection through the official candidate grievance portal.

Objection Fee & Refund Policy:

Candidates pay INR 1,200 per challenged question. If the expert panel upholds your objection, 100% of the fee is refunded to your original payment mode, and a revised Final Answer Key is published.

Question Drop / Ambiguity Rule:

If a question has multiple correct options or typographical ambiguity, it is dropped from calculation. The raw scores of all test-takers in that shift are scaled proportionally across the remaining questions.

How CAT 2026 Normalization & Slot Scaling Works (Slot 1, 2, 3)

CAT slot normalization applies statistical equipercentile equating to harmonize score variations across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3. The formula compares individual slot means and standard deviations against the master pool, with tougher slots receiving upward score calibrations before final percentiles are generated.

1 Calculation of Mean & Standard Deviation

The mean and standard deviation of raw scores are computed for each section in every slot, alongside the top 0.1% candidate scores across all slots.

2 Scaled Score Equating Formula

The candidate raw score is adjusted relative to peer performance across sessions. For instance, if Slot 3 had a harder QA section, candidates in Slot 3 receive an upward adjustment in their scaled score.

3 Percentile Computation Formula

Percentile is calculated as: P = ((N − R) / N) × 100, where N is the total number of test takers and R is the assigned rank based on scaled scores.

Frequently Asked Questions: CAT Scoring, Answer Key & Normalization

How is the CAT 2026 raw score calculated?
CAT 2026 follows a 66-question format with a maximum raw score of 198 marks (24 VARC, 20 DILR, 22 QA). Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) award +3 marks for every correct answer and deduct −1 mark for every incorrect answer. Non-MCQ / TITA (Type In The Answer) questions award +3 marks for correct answers and carry 0 negative marking for wrong or unattempted responses.
How can I check my CAT response sheet and answer key with this calculator?
Once the conducting IIM releases the official candidate response sheet on cdn.digialm.com / iimcat.ac.in, copy your candidate response sheet URL or view page source (Ctrl+U) and paste it into our scanner. The calculator automatically analyzes your MCQ/TITA attempts across VARC, DILR, and QA to compute your raw score, apply slot normalization, and forecast your percentile.
What is the difference between CAT raw score and scaled score?
The raw score is the literal sum of your correct (+3) and incorrect (−1/0) marks out of 198. Because CAT is conducted across Slot 1, Slot 2, and Slot 3 with varying difficulty levels, IIMs employ an equipercentile equating formula (equating mean and standard deviation) to generate the Scaled Score. Your final percentile is calculated strictly from this scaled score.
What CAT score is required for 99+ percentile in top IIMs?
Based on historical trends across 66-question CAT exams, a raw score of roughly 82 to 94 marks out of 198 fetches a 99.0+ percentile. A score of 95 to 109 marks yields a 99.5+ percentile, and a score of 110+ marks generally secures a 99.9+ percentile, qualifying aspirants for IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta interview shortlists.
How do I challenge a question in the provisional CAT answer key?
During the 3-day objection window post-exam, log in to iimcat.ac.in, select Candidate Grievance / Objection tab, choose the Question ID, upload your justification, and pay the fee of INR 1,200 per question. If your objection is valid, the fee is refunded and the final answer key is updated for all candidates.
What happens if a question is dropped in CAT?
If an official CAT question is dropped due to ambiguity or printing error, marks for that question are ignored and the remaining questions are scaled proportionally for all test takers in that specific shift, ensuring no candidate is unfairly penalized.
Is there sectional negative marking in CAT?
Sectional marking is uniform across VARC, DILR, and QA: +3 for correct MCQs, −1 for wrong MCQs, +3 for correct TITA, and 0 for wrong TITA. Candidates must also meet individual sectional cut-offs (usually 70-85+ percentile per section) to qualify for IIM calls.
Which top MBA colleges accept 80 to 90 percentile in CAT?
Candidates scoring between 80 and 90 percentile (raw score of 35 to 55 marks) can target reputed B-schools such as FORE School of Management (Delhi), GIM Goa, TAPMI Manipal, Great Lakes (Chennai/Gurgaon), BIMTECH (Greater Noida), LBSIM (Delhi), K J Somaiya (Mumbai), and LIBA (Chennai).
