Frequently Asked Questions: Online Online M.Com

Clear answers on approvals, eligibility, fees, exams, and career validity.

Who is eligible for an Online M.Com in India?
Candidates with a Bachelor's degree in Commerce (B.Com) or related economics/management disciplines with at least 50% marks from a recognized university.
Can I appear for UGC-NET lectureship with an Online M.Com?
Yes, UGC-DEB recognized online master's degrees are 100% valid for appearing in the UGC-NET exam for Assistant Professor and Junior Research Fellowship (JRF).
What is the total fee for Online M.Com?
Total fees range from ₹60,000 to ₹1,20,000 across 4 semesters, making it one of the most cost-effective post-graduation options in India.
