Frequently Asked Questions About Online Degrees

Get authentic answers regarding approvals, degree legitimacy, placements, and eligibility in India.

Are online degrees approved by UGC-DEB valid for government jobs in India?

Yes, 100% valid. Under the official University Grants Commission (UGC) regulations, degrees acquired through Open & Distance Learning (ODL) and Online mode from recognized Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) are treated as strictly equivalent to degrees earned through conventional on-campus modes. They are recognized for UPSC, SSC, Banking, state civil services, and postgraduate admissions across India.

How do I compare fees, NAAC grades, and placement stats across universities?

OnlineDegrees provides an interactive Side-by-Side Compare Board where you can select up to 3 universities. You can instantly evaluate total course fees, semester EMI schemes, NAAC accreditation grades, LMS features, and live placement recruiting partners.

Are entrance exams like CAT or MAT required for an Online MBA?

Most leading online universities in India do not require CAT, MAT, or XAT scores for admission. Instead, admission is granted on merit based on your graduation percentage (typically 50% for general category, 45% for reserved categories) and relevant work experience. Some universities conduct an internal screening test or aptitude assessment.

Can non-tech graduates switch to software roles with an Online MCA or BCA?

Yes. Non-technical graduates (B.Com, B.A., B.Sc) can enroll in an Online BCA or Online MCA with bridge mathematics or foundational programming modules. The curriculum starts with fundamentals like C, Python, JavaScript, and database systems, accelerating toward full-stack development, AI, and cloud computing.

Are degrees from online universities recognized internationally by WES?

Yes. Prominent universities featured on our portal (such as Amity University Online and Chandigarh University Online) hold World Education Services (WES) verification. This means your credential can be evaluated for Canadian Express Entry, US H1-B education equivalence, and postgraduate admissions in Europe and North America.

How to Verify University Accreditation

Always ensure the university holds an active UGC-DEB approval for the current academic session on the official Distance Education Bureau portal (deb.ugc.ac.in). Technical programs like MBA and MCA must also have AICTE approval, and institutions with NAAC A, A+, or A++ accreditation or NIRF Top 100 rankings guarantee quality education.
