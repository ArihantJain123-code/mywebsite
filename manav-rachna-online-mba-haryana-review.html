Manav Rachna University Online MBA: Fees, Specializations & NCR Corporate Placements

Direct Summary: In-depth review of Manav Rachna University Online MBA in Faridabad, Haryana. Explore specializations in Business Analytics, Marketing, HR, affordable EMI plans, and placement tie-ups across Delhi NCR.

Based in Faridabad, Haryana, Manav Rachna University (MRU) has earned a strong reputation across the National Capital Region (NCR) for technical and management education. With its online wing, Manav Rachna Online offers UGC-DEB and AICTE approved degrees designed to help working professionals balance full-time jobs with flexible academic advancement.

In this review, we examine the Manav Rachna University Online MBA program, covering key highlights, fee options, course modules, and corporate placement support.

Key Features & Approvals

  • NAAC A Grade Institution: Accredited by NAAC with high academic rigor and robust infrastructure.
  • UGC-DEB & AICTE Approved: Fully recognized for online delivery and management education standards.
  • Strong NCR Corporate Ties: Strategically situated near Faridabad and Gurugram industrial corridors.
  • Flexible Batch Timings: Weekend live classes combined with 24/7 LMS portal access.

Program Specializations & Syllabus

Manav Rachna offers multi-disciplinary MBA tracks aligned with top industry demands:

  • Business Analytics: Python for managers, data visualization, business intelligence, and SQL analytics.
  • Marketing & Sales Strategy: Digital sales funnels, SEO/SEM strategies, consumer insights, and retail management.
  • Human Resource Management: HR analytics, strategic talent management, labor welfare, and compensation design.
  • Finance & Accounting: Financial modeling, corporate taxation, managerial accounting, and investment banking basics.

Tuition Fees & Affordable Monthly Installments

Manav Rachna Online MBA provides one of the most balanced pricing structures among NCR private universities:

  • Total Program Fee: Approx ₹96,000 total.
  • Monthly EMI Starts At: Approx ₹2,000/month.
  • No Hidden Costs: Inclusive of study portal access, digital library, and exam evaluations.

LMS & Student Experience

The student LMS portal holds a rating of 4.6/5. Learners benefit from self-assessment quizzes, virtual discussion forums, downloadable course summaries, and proctored online examinations.

Verified Student Testimonial

"Manav Rachna's Online MBA in Business Analytics gave me practical data tools without requiring me to step away from my full-time analyst role in Faridabad. The weekend classes were structured, and the faculty was accessible."
- Pooja Hegde (Online MBA Graduate)

Placement Assistance

Manav Rachna supports students with dedicated interview prep, CV building workshops, and placement referrals. Hiring partners include:

  • Capgemini
  • Microsoft
  • Deloitte
  • Paytm
  • Infosys
  • HCL

Final Verdict

If you live or work in the Delhi NCR region and want a respected NAAC A grade Online MBA under ₹1 Lakh total fee, Manav Rachna University Online MBA is an excellent value recommendation. Get in touch with our team for enrollment guidance!
