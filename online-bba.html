Frequently Asked Questions: Online Online BBA

Clear answers on approvals, eligibility, fees, exams, and career validity.

Can students from Arts or Science streams pursue an Online BBA?
Yes, an Online BBA is open to 10+2 graduates from all academic streams (Commerce, Science, and Arts/Humanities).
What is the average total fee for an Online BBA in India?
Total fees range between ₹90,000 and ₹1,50,000 across 3 years (6 semesters), with no-cost EMI options starting at ₹2,400/month.
Is an Online BBA equivalent to a regular full-time BBA?
Yes, per UGC Regulation 22, online BBA degrees from entitled universities have identical legal equivalence for employment, corporate placements, and postgraduate admissions.
